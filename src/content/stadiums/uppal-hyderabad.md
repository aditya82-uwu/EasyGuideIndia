**Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium** in Uppal, Hyderabad, is home to **Sunrisers Hyderabad** in the IPL and the Hyderabad Cricket Association. It’s right on the Hyderabad Metro, which makes it one of the easier IPL venues to reach. This guide covers the metro, the police-designated public parking, entry rules and the banned-items list (it’s strict, and there’s no cloakroom).

_Malkajgiri / Rachakonda traffic police issue an advisory before each IPL match. Check the latest one for your date._

## How to reach Uppal Stadium

Take the **Hyderabad Metro Blue Line** to **Stadium** station, next to the ground. Traffic police strongly recommend the metro and TGSRTC buses on match days, as heavy vehicles are diverted and roads around Uppal get congested.

## Parking at Uppal Stadium

Traffic police have designated **four public parking areas** within about 500 metres of the stadium:

-   On the **Uppal X Roads–Habsiguda** side: **Penguin** and **TGIALA** grounds
-   On the **Uppal X Roads–Ramanthapur** side: the open space at **DSL (opposite NSL building)** and **Modern Bakery**

Parking inside the stadium is only for VIP/VVIP pass holders. Don’t park on the main road or under the metro, as vehicles are towed and fined.

## Entry rules

-   **Gates open three hours before the start.**
-   For IPL 2026, **digital tickets were mandatory**. Keep your ticket ready in the app.
-   Seats are pre-assigned, so sit in your allotted seat.
-   There’s **no cloakroom**. Banned items are confiscated at the gate and not returned.

## Things not allowed in Uppal Stadium

-   Laptops, cameras, batteries and electronic gadgets (including power banks)
-   Handbags, backpacks and helmets
-   Water bottles and outside food
-   Cigarettes, matchboxes and lighters
-   Writing pens, key chains, ear pads and binoculars
-   Laser pointers, sharp objects, flammable items and weapons
-   Banners and pets
