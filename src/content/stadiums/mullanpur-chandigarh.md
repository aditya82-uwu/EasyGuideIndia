**Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium** in Mullanpur, New Chandigarh, is Punjab Kings’ home ground in the IPL and one of India’s newest international venues. It’s outside Chandigarh city (about 15 km from the centre), so traffic and parking are the main things to plan. This guide covers how to get there, paid parking and booking, entry gates and what you can’t carry.

_Chandigarh and Punjab police issue traffic plans before big matches. Check the latest advisory and your ticket before you travel._

## How to reach Mullanpur stadium

-   **By car / cab:** most fans drive or take a cab from Chandigarh, Mohali or Panchkula. Police have routed match traffic via the New Chandigarh flyover, with the new bypass reserved for official vehicles and some ticket categories. Expect long queues near the stadium, so leave early.
-   **Gates 1–4** are best reached from the Sector 39 (Chandigarh) stadium road side.
-   **Shuttle buses:** for major matches, shuttle services have been run from Chandigarh bus stands. Check the announcement for your match.

## Parking at Mullanpur stadium

-   General public parking is in lots **P4, P5 and P6**. It’s paid, and has been **₹200 for a car** and **₹100 for a two-wheeler**.
-   Pre-book through the **Show My Parking** app to save time at the entry.
-   Don’t park on the approach roads, as police tow vehicles on match days.

## Entry and banned items

-   Use the gate printed on your ticket. The stadium has 13 gates.
-   Gates usually open a couple of hours before the start, so arrive early.
-   As at other IPL venues, don’t carry power banks, bags, outside food or water bottles, coins, lighters, cigarettes, helmets or sharp objects. See our [IPL stadium entry rules guide](/finance/ipl-2026-stadium-entry-rules-mandatory-documents/).
