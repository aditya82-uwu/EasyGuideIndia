## How to reach SMS Stadium

-   **Bus:** the nearest stops are **Rambagh Circle** and **Nagar Nigam (Lal Kothi)**.
-   **Train:** **Gandhinagar Jaipur** is the closest railway station. From **Jaipur Junction**, take a cab or auto.
-   **Metro:** Jaipur Metro doesn’t stop at the stadium, so you’ll need an auto or cab from the nearest station.
-   **Cab / auto:** expect diversions near the stadium. Get dropped a short walk away.

## Parking near SMS Stadium

Parking is limited. Traffic police enforce a **no-parking ring** around the stadium and ban heavy vehicles on match days. Follow the parking signs on the day, use designated public lots, and allow time to walk from your parking to your gate.

## Entry and banned items

-   Your gate and stand are printed on your ticket. Different stands use different gates.
-   Reach the stadium early; security checks take time for evening matches.
-   **Not allowed:** bags, bottles, cameras, lighters and outside food, plus power banks, coins, cigarettes, helmets and sharp objects as at other IPL venues. See our [IPL stadium entry rules guide](/finance/ipl-2026-stadium-entry-rules-mandatory-documents/).
