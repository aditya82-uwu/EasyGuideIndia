**Estadio Azteca**, now officially known by its sponsored name **Estadio Banorte**, is Mexico City’s giant football stadium in Coyoacán. It is home to Club América and the Mexico national team, and it’s the only stadium to have hosted matches at three FIFA World Cups (1970, 1986 and 2026). This guide covers what you need for any match or concert: how to get there on the Tren Ligero, parking, what you can and can’t bring, and payments.

_Information below is from the stadium’s official A–Z guide. Rules can change for individual events, so check [estadiobanorte.com.mx](https://www.estadiobanorte.com.mx/en) before you go._

## How to get to Estadio Azteca by public transport

The **Tren Ligero** (light rail) is the easiest way for big events:

1.  Take **Metro Line 2** (blue line) to its southern end, **Tasqueña**.
2.  Transfer to the **Tren Ligero** heading south.
3.  Get off at **Estadio Azteca** station, right outside the stadium.

You pay for the Tren Ligero with Mexico City’s integrated mobility card, and trains get very crowded before and after big matches, so leave plenty of time. The stadium can also be reached by bus along Calzada de Tlalpan.

## Parking at Estadio Azteca

The stadium’s general parking has about **3,200 spaces** with several vehicle entrances, but for some 2026 matches, including Mexico vs Portugal and América vs Cruz Azul, no parking was available. It fills up quickly for Club América and national team matches, and traffic on Calzada de Tlalpan is heavy on event days. Prices and pre-booking vary by event, so check the event page or your ticketing platform before driving.

## Entry and gates

-   The stadium has **11 gates**. Your assigned gate is printed on your ticket, so check it before you arrive.
-   Bring your **digital or physical ticket in good condition**.
-   For Liga MX matches, Mexican football’s **Fan ID** registration may be required. Check with the ticket seller.

## What you can’t bring into Estadio Azteca

The official list is long. These are the items fans most often get caught out by:

-   **Battery chargers and power banks**
-   Outside food, drinks and liquids of any kind, and bottles, cans, thermoses or cups not bought inside
-   Cameras or binoculars with lenses longer than 15 cm, video cameras, tripods and selfie sticks
-   Laptops and laptop cases, luggage, and motorcycle helmets
-   Canes and sticks (support canes are allowed), and large hats
-   Horns, whistles, rattles, megaphones and air horns
-   Masks of any kind, and banners or signs that block others’ view
-   Balls and inflatables (only deflated balls bought at the stadium stores on match day)
-   Strollers (unless medically necessary and tagged at designated gates)
-   Smoke canisters, fireworks, flares, laser pointers and drones
-   Cigarettes and vapes: the stadium is 100% smoke- and vape-free, including the area around the entrances

## Food, payments and services

-   **Payments:** concession stands accept both **cash and card**.
-   **Medical help:** paramedics, first aid and ambulances are on hand during events.
-   **Accessibility:** services are available for guests with reduced mobility. Contact the stadium in advance if you need assistance.
-   **Lost and found:** items lost inside the stadium are kept temporarily in a designated area.
-   **Stadium tours:** check the official website, as tours were suspended during the renovation.
