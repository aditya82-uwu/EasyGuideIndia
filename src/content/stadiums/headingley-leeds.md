**Headingley** in Leeds is home to Yorkshire County Cricket Club and hosts England Tests and white-ball internationals, including India’s tours of England. It’s famous for the lively Western Terrace. This guide covers how to get there by train and park and ride, bag size, and the food and alcohol rules.

_Check the official [Yorkshire CCC match-day information](https://yorkshireccc.com/ground-regs) for your fixture._

## How to get to Headingley

-   **Train:** **Burley Park** station is about a 5-minute walk from the St Michael’s Lane entrance. Headingley station is also nearby.
-   **Park and ride:** on international match days, Leeds City Council runs a park and ride from **Beckett Park**, about 800 m north of the ground.

## Bags

Bags must be no bigger than **45 × 30 × 30 cm (18″ × 12″ × 12″)** and fit under a seat. There’s **no luggage drop** at Headingley.

## Food, drink and alcohol

| Match type | Bring your own alcohol? |
| --- | --- |
| Internationals, T20 Blast, Charlotte Edwards Cup, The Hundred | No |
| County Championship and One-Day Cup | Up to 4 × 440 ml cans of beer/lager _or_ one 70 cl container of wine |

You can bring refillable water bottles, soft drinks in sealed plastic containers, or up to 2 litres of water in sealed plastic bottles. Inside, there’s a wide range of food, including curry and vegetarian options.
