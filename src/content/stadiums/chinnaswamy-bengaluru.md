## How to reach Chinnaswamy Stadium

-   **Metro:** **Cubbon Park** and **MG Road** stations on Namma Metro’s Purple Line are both within walking distance.
-   **Bus:** BMTC runs buses from the stadium after IPL matches.
-   **Cabs and autos:** Ola, Uber and auto pick-up/drop-off points have been at **BRV Ground** and **Manekshaw Parade Ground**.

## Parking near Chinnaswamy Stadium

-   **Designated parking** in 2026 included **UB City** and **Shivajinagar Bus Stand** (police list), and **Freedom Park** in earlier seasons.
-   **No parking** on Queens Road, MG Road, Link Road, Raj Bhavan Road, T Chowdaiah Road, Race Course Road, Central Street, Cubbon Road, St Mark’s Road, Museum Road, Kasturba Road, Lavelle Road and Vittal Mallya Road, and none inside Cubbon Park.

## Things not allowed in Chinnaswamy Stadium

-   Power banks, pen drives, laptops, tablets and other electronic gadgets
-   Backpacks, bottles, thermos flasks and outside food
-   Selfie sticks, umbrellas, helmets and pens
-   Perfumes, gels, toys and toy guns
-   Cigarettes, bidis, gutka and supari
