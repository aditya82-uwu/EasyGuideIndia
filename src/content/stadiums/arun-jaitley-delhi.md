**Arun Jaitley Stadium** (formerly **Feroz Shah Kotla**) on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg in central Delhi is the home of Delhi Capitals in the IPL and WPL, and one of India’s oldest Test grounds. It’s run by the Delhi & District Cricket Association (DDCA) and holds about 41,000 spectators. The roads around it (BSZ Marg, JLN Marg and Asaf Ali Road) are among Delhi’s busiest, so on match days you need to know which road leads to your gate, where to park, and which metro station to use.

_Details below are from Delhi Traffic Police advisories for IPL 2026 matches. Arrangements are announced before every big match, so check the latest @dtptraffic advisory for your date._

## Nearest metro station to Arun Jaitley Stadium

**Delhi Gate** metro station on the **Violet Line** is closest, just a short walk from the stadium’s gates on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg. Delhi Traffic Police strongly advises fans to use the metro on match days, as roads around the stadium are closed or diverted from late afternoon until around midnight.

## Which road leads to your gate

Your gate number is printed on your ticket. The traffic police split entry routes by gate:

| Gate numbers | Approach from |
| --- | --- |
| Gates 1–8 | Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg (south side) |
| Gates 10–15 | Jawaharlal Nehru Marg, near Ambedkar Stadium bus terminal (east side) |
| Gates 16–18 | Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, near the petrol pump (west side) |

## Parking near Arun Jaitley Stadium

-   **General public (free):** **Mata Sundari Road**, **Rajghat Power House Road** and **Velodrome Road**, from where you walk or use park-and-ride.
-   **Vehicles with an official parking label only:** P1 (JP Park, near Gate 3), P2 (Vikram Nagar, near Shahidi Park), P3 (near the JJB Prayas office, two-wheelers) and P4 (between Gates 7 and 8).
-   **No parking:** Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, Jawaharlal Nehru Marg, and the Ring Road stretch between Rajghat and the IP Flyover. Illegally parked vehicles are towed and prosecuted.

## Cab and auto drop-off points

-   **Gate 2**, near Maulana Azad Medical College on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg
-   **Rajghat Chowk**
-   The **Ring Road service lane** between the IP Flyover and Rajghat

## Roads to avoid on match days

From about 5 pm until midnight on evening match days, avoid Jawaharlal Nehru Marg (Rajghat to Delhi Gate), Asaf Ali Road (Turkman Gate to Delhi Gate) and Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg (Delhi Gate to ITO). Heavy vehicles are restricted on these stretches.

## Tickets, entry and banned items

-   **Tickets:** buy only from Delhi Capitals’ official ticketing partner for the season. Follow the entry instructions on your ticket (e-ticket QR or physical ticket) and use the gate printed on it.
-   **Arrive early:** gates open well before the start, and security queues on BSZ Marg are long for evening matches.
-   **Commonly banned at IPL venues:** bags and backpacks, power banks and chargers, outside food and water bottles, coins, lighters and cigarettes, helmets, umbrellas, and anything sharp. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the nearest metro station to Arun Jaitley Stadium?

Delhi Gate on the Violet Line, a short walk from the gates on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg.

### Where can I park for a match at Arun Jaitley Stadium?

Free public parking has been provided on Mata Sundari Road, Rajghat Power House Road and Velodrome Road. Parking near the gates is only for vehicles with official labels.

### How do I reach Gates 16, 17 and 18?

Gates 16–18 are approached from Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg on the west side, near the petrol pump. Gates 10–15 are on the Jawaharlal Nehru Marg side, and Gates 1–8 are on BSZ Marg’s south side.

### Where do Uber and Ola drop off near Arun Jaitley Stadium?

Near Gate 2 by Maulana Azad Medical College, at Rajghat Chowk, or on the Ring Road service lane between the IP Flyover and Rajghat.
