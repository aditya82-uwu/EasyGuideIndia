**Edgbaston** in Birmingham is home to Warwickshire County Cricket Club (the Bears) and one of England’s loudest international venues. India matches here draw huge support from the local British-Indian community. It hosts Tests, white-ball internationals, T20 Blast Finals Day and The Hundred. This guide covers the shuttle bus from New Street, parking, bags, and food and alcohol rules.

_Check the official [Edgbaston travel page](https://edgbaston.com/visiting-us/travel/) and [ground regulations](https://www.edgbaston.com/ground-regulations) for your match._

## How to get to Edgbaston

-   **501 Shuttle Bus:** picks up outside **Birmingham New Street station** on Smallbrook Queensway (near the Bullring). A day ticket was £6 per adult and £4 per child (aged 5 to 15) in April 2026, bought in the NXBus mTicket app or by contactless on the bus. The shuttle runs for selected matches, including all internationals and The Hundred.
-   **Regular buses:** National Express West Midlands routes **45 and 47** run along Pershore Road, a short walk from the ground. Routes 1, 61, 63 and 35 also stop nearby.

## Parking

Book official parking in advance at the off-site car parks. Streets around the ground carry local parking restrictions on match days, so use the pre-booked car parks rather than parking on residential roads.

## Bags, food and alcohol

-   **Bags:** avoid large bags if possible. All bags and spectators are searched on arrival.
-   **Food:** you can bring your own food and non-alcoholic drinks, but no glass bottles.
-   **Alcohol:** **not allowed** for international matches, T20 Blast (including Finals Day) and The Hundred. For County Championship and One-Day Cup matches, _members_ may bring up to four cans of beer or one bottle of wine.
