The **Bristol County Ground** on Nevil Road, currently called the **Seat Unique Stadium** for sponsorship reasons, is home to Gloucestershire County Cricket Club. It hosts county cricket plus England white-ball internationals, men’s and women’s. This guide covers how to get there by bus and train and what to expect on match day.

_Check [Gloucestershire Cricket’s ground regulations](https://www.gloscricket.co.uk/ground-regulations/) for bag and food rules for your match._

## How to get to the Bristol County Ground

-   **Bus:** services **17, 70, 72, 73, 74, 75 and 76** stop within about a five-minute walk (72 to 76 on Gloucester Road, 17 on Muller Road). Check First Bus for times.
-   **Train:** **Bristol Temple Meads** is about 2 miles away and **Bristol Parkway** about 4 miles. The local Montpelier station is closer, with a short bus ride or walk from there.
-   **Car:** the ground is in a residential area with little parking, so the club encourages public transport.

## Food, drink and bags

There’s a good mix of food and drink on the concourses. Rules on bringing your own food, drink and alcohol, and on bag sizes, vary by match type (international or county), so check Gloucestershire Cricket’s ground regulations before you go.
