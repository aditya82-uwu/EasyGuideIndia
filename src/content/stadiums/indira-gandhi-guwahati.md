The **Indira Gandhi Athletic Stadium**, also called **Sarusajai Stadium**, is in the Sarusajai Sports Complex on the southern edge of Guwahati, beside NH-37. It’s home to **NorthEast United FC** in the Indian Super League and hosts athletics and big events. This guide covers how to get there, parking and entry tips.

## Sarusajai Stadium at a glance

## How to reach Sarusajai Stadium

-   The complex is beside **NH-37** (the Guwahati bypass) in Lokhra, southern Guwahati.
-   Guwahati’s city buses, autos and app cabs all serve the area. App cabs are usually the most convenient for evening matches.
-   From Guwahati Railway Station or the airport, take a cab directly to the Sarusajai Sports Complex.

## Parking and entry

-   Parking inside the complex is generally for pass holders. For big matches, traffic police announce public parking zones nearby, so check the advisory.
-   Your gate and stand are printed on your ticket.
-   Don’t carry outside food, bottles, flares, fireworks, lighters or sharp objects.
