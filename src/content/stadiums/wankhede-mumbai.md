## How to reach Wankhede Stadium

-   **Local train:** **Churchgate** (Western Railway) is the closest, a few minutes’ walk. **Marine Lines** is one stop before and also walkable. From Central Railway, get off at **CSMT** and take a cab or walk (about 20–25 minutes).
-   **Metro:** Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) also serves the Churchgate area.
-   **Cab / taxi:** expect diversions and slow traffic on Marine Drive before and after the match. Get dropped a little away from the stadium and walk.

## Parking near Wankhede Stadium

There’s **no parking at the stadium** for the general public, and roadside parking around Churchgate and Marine Drive is restricted on match days. If you must drive, park in a paid public parking facility elsewhere in South Mumbai and take a train or cab for the last stretch. Public transport is by far the easier option.

## Entry gates and tickets

-   **Your gate is printed on your ticket.** Wankhede’s entry gates are spread around the ground, some on the Marine Drive side and some on the Churchgate / D Road side. Check which side your gate is on before you arrive to avoid walking all the way around.
-   **Tickets:** buy only from Mumbai Indians’ official ticketing partner (BookMyShow for IPL 2026). Follow the entry instructions on your ticket.
-   **Arrive early:** gates usually open a couple of hours before the start, and security queues are long for evening matches.

## Things not allowed in Wankhede Stadium

-   Power banks
-   Backpacks and large bags (carry only a phone, wallet and ticket)
-   Outside food and liquids, including water bottles
-   Professional cameras
-   Helmets
-   Coins
-   Sharp objects, lighters and cigarettes
