## How to reach Narendra Modi Stadium by metro

**Motera Stadium** metro station on Ahmedabad Metro’s **Red Line** (north–south corridor) is right by the stadium. It’s usually the fastest way to arrive and, especially, to leave, as road traffic after a full-house match takes a long time to clear.

## Narendra Modi Stadium parking

-   **Book parking in advance** on the **Show My Parking** app. On-the-spot availability is limited.
-   Parking is organised in zones around the stadium, and your entry gate decides which zone is most convenient. Check your ticket’s gate before booking.
-   Leave extra time after the match, as exiting the car and bike parking areas can be slow.

## Entry rules

-   **Gates opened about 3 hours before the start in IPL 2025.** Arrive early; security checks and frisking take time.
-   Carry a **valid, undamaged ticket** and use the gate printed on it.
-   **No re-entry** once you leave, unless explicitly permitted.

## What is not allowed in Narendra Modi Stadium

-   Bags and briefcases (other than small ladies’ purses), and motorcycle helmets
-   Power banks and charging devices (not named in the GCA list we found, but commonly banned at IPL venues, so leave them at home)
-   Outside food and drinks; bottles, tins, cans and metal containers
-   Cameras, binoculars and recording devices
-   Lighters, firecrackers and cigarettes (smoking is banned inside)
-   Musical instruments, horns and noisemakers
-   Umbrellas, selfie sticks, weapons and sharp objects
