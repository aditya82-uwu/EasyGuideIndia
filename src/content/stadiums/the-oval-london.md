**The Kia Oval** in Kennington, south London, is home to Surrey County Cricket Club and traditionally hosts the final Test of England’s summer, often including India’s tours. It also hosts T20 and one-day internationals and The Hundred’s Oval Invincibles. This guide covers how to get there, bag size, food and alcohol rules, and match-day tips.

_Check the official [Kia Oval ground regulations](https://www.kiaoval.com/ground-regulations/) and [match-day guide](https://www.kiaoval.com/match-day-guide/) for your fixture._

## How to get to the Oval

-   **Oval** tube station (Northern line) is right by the ground.
-   **Vauxhall** (Victoria line and National Rail) is a short walk away.
-   Driving isn’t recommended, as there’s no spectator parking and the area is busy on match days.

## Bag size

One bag per person, no bigger than **45 cm × 30 cm × 30 cm (18″ × 12″ × 12″)**, so it fits under your seat. All bags are searched.

## Food and alcohol rules

| Match type | Bring your own alcohol? |
| --- | --- |
| Major internationals (Category A, e.g. ODIs) | No |
| The Hundred | No |
| County Championship and One-Day Cup | Limited: up to four 500 ml cans of beer/lager _or_ one 750 ml plastic container of wine |

You **can bring your own food** to all matches, which makes a picnic a cheap option for a long Test day.
