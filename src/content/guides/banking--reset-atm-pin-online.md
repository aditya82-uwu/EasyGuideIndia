Forgot your debit card (ATM) PIN, or want to change it? You don’t usually need to visit the branch. You can set a new PIN through your **bank’s app**, **net banking** or **at an ATM** using an OTP on your registered mobile.

## Method 1: Bank app or net banking

1.  Log in to your bank’s official app or net banking.
2.  Go to **Cards → Debit Card → Set / Reset PIN** (the name varies by bank).
3.  Select your card and verify with the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
4.  Enter and confirm the new 4-digit PIN.

## Method 2: At your bank’s ATM (Green PIN)

1.  Insert your card at your own bank’s ATM.
2.  Choose **PIN Generation / Forgot PIN / Green PIN**.
3.  Enter your registered mobile number or account number as asked, then the OTP you receive.
4.  Set the new PIN.

## Method 3: Phone banking

Many banks let you generate a PIN via the customer care IVR with your registered mobile. Call the number on the back of your card.

## PIN safety tips

-   Don’t use your birth year or 1234.
-   Cover the keypad when entering your PIN.
-   No bank employee will ever ask for your PIN.
