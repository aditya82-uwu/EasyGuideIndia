## How the auction works

-   **Purse.** Each franchise can spend only what is left of its purse. Under the IPL player regulations for 2025 to 2027, the total salary cap for 2027 is ₹157 crore, made up of the auction purse, incremental performance pay and match fees.
-   **Squad limit.** Reports say a squad can have up to 25 players and up to 8 overseas players. An open slot is a spot left in the squad.
-   **Base price.** Each player chooses a base price, and bidding starts there. In the 2026 auction the lowest base price for uncapped Indian players was ₹30 lakh.
-   **Bidding.** Franchises raise their bids until one team is left. That team signs the player at that price, which comes out of its purse.
-   **Right to Match.** Reports say RTM cards are not used at mini-auctions. Confirm with the IPL if this matters for a particular player.

## Who can enter the auction

Players do not apply directly. Indian players are registered through their state cricket association, and the BCCI then shortlists the final auction pool. Overseas players come through their national boards. There is no public IPL registration form, and anyone selling one is not connected with the BCCI or a franchise.
