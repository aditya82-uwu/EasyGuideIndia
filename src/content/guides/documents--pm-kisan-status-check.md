Under **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)**, eligible farmer families get **₹6,000 a year**, paid in three instalments of **₹2,000** directly into their bank account. You can check whether your instalments have been paid, and why one might be stuck, on the official PM-Kisan website using your Aadhaar-linked mobile number or registration number.

## How to check PM Kisan beneficiary status

1.  Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/).
2.  In the Farmers Corner, click **Know Your Status**.
3.  Enter your **registration number**. If you don’t know it, click **Know your registration number** and find it with your mobile or Aadhaar number.
4.  Enter the captcha and the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
5.  You’ll see your details, **eKYC status**, **land seeding**, **Aadhaar–bank seeding**, and every instalment with its payment status.

## What to check if an instalment is missing

| Shown on status page | What it means | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eKYC: No | eKYC isn’t done, so payments stop | Complete eKYC (OTP on pmkisan.gov.in, face authentication in the PM-Kisan app, or biometric at a CSC) |
| Land seeding: No | Land records aren’t verified | Contact your block / agriculture office or patwari |
| Aadhaar bank seeding: No / inactive | No bank account is linked to Aadhaar for DBT | Get your Aadhaar [seeded with your bank](/documents/aadhaar-seeding-status-check/) |
| Payment failed / rejected | Account closed, name mismatch, or bank issue | Update bank details with your bank / agriculture office |

## How to complete PM Kisan eKYC

-   **OTP eKYC:** on pmkisan.gov.in, under Farmers Corner, choose **eKYC** and verify with your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
-   **Face authentication:** using the official **PM-KISAN mobile app**.
-   **Biometric eKYC:** at a Common Service Centre (CSC).

## When is the next PM Kisan instalment?

Instalments are paid roughly every four months. The government announces each release date officially, usually a few days in advance. Check [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) or official announcements, and ignore social media messages asking you to “register” or pay a fee.
