Before booking, check how many seats are available, or how long the waitlist is, on your train and class. You can do it on IRCTC or Indian Railways’ enquiry site without logging in. Here’s how, and what the availability codes mean.

## How to check seat availability

1.  Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) or the IRCTC Rail Connect app (or [indianrail.gov.in](https://www.indianrail.gov.in/) → Seat Availability).
2.  Enter **From** and **To** stations and the **journey date**, and choose the quota (General, Tatkal, Ladies, Senior Citizen, etc.).
3.  In the train list, click a **class** to see availability for the next few days.

## What the availability shows

| Shown as | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| AVAILABLE-0045 | 45 seats/berths available |
| RAC 12 | All berths booked; you’d get RAC number 12 |
| GNWL 30/WL 20 | Waitlisted; you’d be WL 20 |
| REGRET / NOT AVAILABLE | Waitlist is full; no booking possible |

## Booking tips

-   Bookings open **60 days in advance** (excluding the journey date).
-   If a train is full, try Tatkal (see [Tatkal booking](/railway/irctc-tatkal-ticket-booking/)) or [special trains](/railway/how-to-find-indian-railways-special-trains-booking-guide/).
