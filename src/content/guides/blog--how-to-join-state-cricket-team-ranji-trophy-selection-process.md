To **join a state cricket team** in India and play the Ranji Trophy, you need to come up through your state association’s system: an affiliated club or academy, district trials, inter-district tournaments, state age-group teams, and then the senior squad. This guide explains each stage, the BCCI tournaments at each age, and the documents you’ll need.

## Quick answers

-   **Age limit:** age-group tournaments use a cut-off date set by the BCCI each season. The senior Ranji Trophy has no official upper age limit that we found, so confirm with your state association.
-   **Academy needed?** Not a rule we found. What matters is playing official matches for an affiliated club or district team.
-   **Fee:** official district and state trials are free or charge a small association fee. Do not pay anyone who promises selection.
-   **Who selects?** Your state association's selectors, not the BCCI or an IPL franchise.

## 1\. Join an affiliated club or academy

Each state association (for example DDCA in Delhi, MCA in Mumbai, CAB in Bengal, TNCA in Tamil Nadu, KSCA in Karnataka) runs club leagues through its district units. Play for a club or academy **affiliated** to your district association, so your matches are official and your performances are recorded. Most state associations have tiered divisions. Performing in higher divisions gets you noticed.

## 2\. District trials and inter-district tournaments

-   District associations hold **selection trials** for under-14, under-16, under-19, under-23 and senior teams, usually before the domestic season. Watch your district and state association’s official website and notice board.
-   District teams play **inter-district tournaments**. State selectors pick probables from these.
-   Probables attend a **state camp** with fitness tests and practice matches before the final squad is named.

## 3\. BCCI tournaments by age group

| Age group | Men’s BCCI tournaments |
| --- | --- |
| Under-16 | Vijay Merchant Trophy |
| Under-19 | Cooch Behar Trophy (multi-day), Vinoo Mankad Trophy (one-day) |
| Under-23 | CK Nayudu Trophy |
| Senior | Ranji Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy, Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy |

The BCCI also runs a full women’s domestic calendar (under-15, under-19, under-23 and senior one-day and T20 competitions), with the same state-association route.

## 4\. Documents and age verification

-   **Birth certificate** (ideally registered soon after birth), plus school records showing the same date of birth.
-   **Aadhaar** with matching name and date of birth.
-   For age-group cricket, the BCCI’s age-verification programme can include a **medical bone-age test**. Players found to have falsified age face bans, so never alter documents.
-   Most associations need **domicile or residence proof** for the state, or a no-objection certificate if you’re moving from another association.

## 5\. Fitness and skills selectors look for

-   Consistent performance across a season, not one-off scores.
-   Fitness: state teams run fitness tests (such as timed runs and the yo-yo test). Standards vary by association.
-   A clear role: a specialist batter, bowler, wicketkeeper or all-rounder.
-   Match temperament, fielding and discipline.

## 6\. From state cricket to the IPL and India

Performances in the Ranji Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy and especially the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy (T20) are closely watched by IPL scouts and national selectors. See [how to join the IPL](/blog/how-to-join-ipl-cricket-trials-selection-process/).
