During festivals (Diwali, Chhath, Holi), summer holidays and big events, Indian Railways runs **special trains** on busy routes. They’re bookable on IRCTC like regular trains, but they’re announced at short notice and often carry **special fares**. Here’s how to find and book them.

## How to identify a special train

-   Most special trains have **5-digit numbers starting with 0** (for example 0xxxx).
-   They appear in normal IRCTC search results once notified, often marked “Special” or with special fare.

## How to find special trains

1.  Search your route and date on [IRCTC](https://www.irctc.co.in/). Notified specials show up with regular trains.
2.  Check the **zonal railway’s** website and official social media (for example Central Railway or Northern Railway), where specials are announced first.
3.  Use [NTES](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/) to see running days and schedules.

## Booking special trains

-   Booking is done the same way as a regular train on IRCTC.
-   The **advance booking period** can be shorter than the usual 60 days, as specials are notified later.
-   Fares may be **special fares**, higher than regular trains on the same route.
-   Tatkal quota may or may not be available, depending on the train.
