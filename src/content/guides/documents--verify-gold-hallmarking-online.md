In India, hallmarked gold jewellery must carry a **6-digit HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification)** code. You can verify any HUID for free with the Bureau of Indian Standards’ **BIS CARE** app to confirm the jewellery’s purity and where it was hallmarked, before you buy or when you sell.

## What a valid hallmark includes

-   The **BIS standard mark** (triangle logo)
-   **Purity / fineness** grade, for example 22K916, 18K750 or 14K585
-   A **6-digit alphanumeric HUID** code

## How to verify HUID with the BIS CARE app

1.  Install the official **BIS CARE** app from the Play Store or App Store.
2.  Register with your name, mobile number and OTP.
3.  Open **Verify HUID**.
4.  Enter the 6-digit HUID code engraved on the jewellery.
5.  The app shows the article type, purity, the jeweller’s registration and the hallmarking centre that tested it.

## If the HUID doesn’t match

-   If the purity or article shown doesn’t match what you’re buying, don’t buy it and ask the jeweller to explain.
-   You can file a complaint through the BIS CARE app.
-   Always ask for a bill showing the HUID, weight, purity and making charges.
