An **IPL scout** watches domestic and age-group cricket to find players a franchise might sign or invite to trials. There’s no formal exam or licence for the job. Most scouts are former first-class players, coaches or analysts who build a track record and a network. This guide explains what scouts do, the skills they need, and realistic routes into the role.

## What does an IPL scout do?

-   **Watches cricket:** the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy, Ranji Trophy, age-group tournaments and state T20 leagues such as the TNPL and Maharaja Trophy.
-   **Writes scouting reports:** strengths, weaknesses, role fit and potential, for the franchise’s coaches and management.
-   **Uses data:** strike rates, phase-wise economy, match-ups, and ball-tracking or video where available.
-   **Arranges trials:** recommends players for franchise trials before the auction.

## Skills you need

-   A strong technical understanding of batting, bowling and fielding.
-   An eye for potential, not just current performance.
-   Comfort with cricket data and video analysis tools.
-   Clear, honest report writing.
-   Willingness to travel through a long domestic season.

## Routes into scouting

1.  **Playing or coaching background:** many scouts played first-class or age-group cricket, or coach at academies and associations.
2.  **Coaching qualifications:** BCCI and state association coaching courses (run through the National Cricket Academy / Centre of Excellence and associations) add credibility.
3.  **Performance analysis:** analysts who work with state teams, T20 leagues or franchises often move into talent identification.
4.  **Start local:** scout for clubs, academies or state T20 league teams, and build a record of players you identified early.
5.  **Network:** franchise talent teams hire through contacts and track record rather than public job ads, though some openings are posted on franchise and sports-job websites.

## Is scouting a full-time job?

A few senior roles at franchises are full-time, but many scouts work on seasonal or part-time contracts alongside coaching or analysis. Pay varies widely by franchise and experience, and isn’t publicly disclosed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to have played professional cricket?

It helps a lot, but it isn’t strictly required. A strong analysis or coaching record can also get you in.

### How do players get scouted?

By performing in recognised domestic cricket. See [how to join the IPL](/blog/how-to-join-ipl-cricket-trials-selection-process/) and [how to join a state team](/blog/how-to-join-state-cricket-team-ranji-trophy-selection-process/).
