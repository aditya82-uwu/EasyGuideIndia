The **Staff Selection Commission (SSC)** publishes results for CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, CPO, Stenographer, JE and other exams on its official website, **ssc.gov.in**. Results come out stage by stage (Tier 1, Tier 2, final) as PDFs listing the roll numbers of qualified candidates, and scorecards appear later in the candidate login. Here’s how to check.

## How to check SSC result

1.  Go to [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in/).
2.  Open the **Result** section and choose your exam (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, etc.).
3.  Download the result PDF for your exam and stage.
4.  Press Ctrl+F (or Find in page on mobile) and search for your **roll number** or name.
5.  If your roll number is listed, you’ve qualified for the next stage (or been selected, for final results).

## How to check your SSC marks / scorecard

1.  Log in to the candidate section on ssc.gov.in with your registration number and password.
2.  Open **Result / Marks** for your exam once SSC activates the scorecard link (usually a few days after the result).
3.  Download or print your scorecard. The link is available for a limited period only.

## What’s in the result PDF

-   Roll numbers (and often names) of qualified candidates
-   Category-wise **cut-off marks**
-   Number of candidates qualified in each category
