The **PM Kisan beneficiary list** shows every farmer in your village who is registered under PM-KISAN. You can check it online without logging in, to see whether your name is on it. If it isn’t, you’ll need to register or fix your records.

## How to see the village-wise beneficiary list

1.  Go to [pmkisan.gov.in](https://pmkisan.gov.in/).
2.  In the Farmers Corner, click **Beneficiary List**.
3.  Select your **State**, **District**, **Sub-District**, **Block** and **Village**.
4.  Click **Get Report**.
5.  The list shows beneficiary names (often with father’s name and gender). Use Ctrl+F on a computer to search for your name.

## Your name is on the list, but no money arrived?

Being on the list means you’re registered. Payment also needs eKYC, land seeding and Aadhaar–bank seeding. [Check your PM Kisan beneficiary status](/documents/pm-kisan-status-check/) to see exactly what’s pending.

## Your name isn’t on the list?

-   Search with small spelling variations. Names in the list may be in English transliteration.
-   If you haven’t registered, use **New Farmer Registration** on pmkisan.gov.in or visit a CSC.
-   If you registered but you’re missing, contact your block agriculture office or the PM-Kisan helpline.
