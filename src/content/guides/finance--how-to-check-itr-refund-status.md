You filed your return and the money has not arrived. The first step is to check where the refund stands, and that takes a few minutes on the income tax portal. This guide shows how, what the status means and what to fix if it is stuck.

## Before you check

-   Your return must be **e-verified**. A filed but unverified return is not processed, so no refund is issued.
-   Keep your **PAN** and the **assessment year** (AY) ready. The AY is the year after the income year: income from FY 2025-26 goes in **AY 2026-27**.
-   Your **bank account** should be pre-validated on the portal and linked to your PAN.

## Check refund status on the e-filing portal

1.  Go to [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) and log in with your PAN, password and captcha.
2.  Open **e-File**, then **Income Tax Returns**, then **View Filed Returns**.
3.  Select the right assessment year and open **View Details** on your return.
4.  The status line shows where the return is, from verification to processing to refund issued or demand raised. Menu names can change, so look for the same options if the labels differ.

## Check without logging in

You can also check with your PAN and assessment year on the refund status page of the NSDL (Protean) tax information site. Enter the PAN, pick the AY, add the captcha and submit. This shows whether a refund was issued, and where it was sent, but not the full processing history.

## What the status means

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Return filed, not verified | e-verify it. Nothing moves until you do |
| Under processing | The department is checking the return against your tax records. Wait, then check again |
| Refund issued or sent to bank | The money is on its way to your account. Banks may take a short while to show it |
| Refund failed | The credit failed, often because of a bank account problem. Fix the account and request a reissue |
| Demand raised | Tax is payable. Open the intimation, check the figures and respond on the portal |

## Why the refund is stuck

-   **Not e-verified.** Verify the return with Aadhaar OTP, net banking or another option on the portal.
-   **Bank account not validated or not matching.** Add the account, validate it, and make sure the name matches your PAN record.
-   **PAN not linked with Aadhaar.** Check it with our [PAN Aadhaar link status guide](/documents/check-pan-aadhaar-link-status/).
-   **Adjustment against an old demand.** If you owe tax from an earlier year, the refund can be adjusted against it. The intimation shows this.
-   **Mismatch with Form 26AS or AIS.** If TDS or income in your return differs from what the tax records show, the return can be adjusted or held. You can view both after logging in to the portal.

## If the refund failed

Update and validate the right bank account on the portal, then raise a **refund reissue** request under the service options in the e-filing account. The exact path can change, so use the portal help page for your account, or call the e-filing helpline.

## Interest on delayed refunds

The Act provides for interest on some refunds that are paid late. Whether it applies, and how much, depends on your case and shows in the intimation. See the notice rather than relying on a general rule.
