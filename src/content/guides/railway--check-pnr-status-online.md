Your **PNR (Passenger Name Record)** is the 10-digit number on your train ticket. With it, you can check whether your ticket is confirmed, RAC or waitlisted, and after the chart is prepared, your coach and berth numbers.

## How to check PNR status

-   **IRCTC:** log in and open your booking, or use **PNR Enquiry** on [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) / the Rail Connect app.
-   **Indian Railways enquiry:** use PNR Enquiry on [indianrail.gov.in](https://www.indianrail.gov.in/).
-   **SMS:** send **PNR <10-digit PNR>** to **139**.
-   **Phone:** call **139** and follow the IVR.

## What PNR status codes mean

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CNF | Confirmed, with a berth allotted |
| RAC | Reservation Against Cancellation: you can travel, sharing a side-lower berth, and may get a full berth |
| WL / GNWL | Waitlisted (General waitlist): the best chance of confirmation |
| RLWL / PQWL | Remote Location / Pooled Quota waitlist: generally lower chances |
| TQWL | [Tatkal](/railway/irctc-tatkal-ticket-booking/) quota waitlist |
| CAN | Cancelled |

## When is the chart prepared?

The first reservation chart is usually prepared several hours before departure (Railways moved the first chart earlier in 2025, about 8 hours before departure for most trains). Your final berth appears on PNR status after charting.
