**UPI** lets you send and receive money instantly from your bank account using just a phone. Setting it up takes a few minutes. You can set your **UPI PIN** with your **debit card**, or with **Aadhaar OTP** if you don’t have a debit card. Here’s how.

## What you need

-   A bank account with your **mobile number registered**
-   That SIM in the phone you’re setting up UPI on
-   A debit card for the account, **or** Aadhaar linked to the account and mobile

## How to set up UPI

1.  Install a UPI app: BHIM, your bank’s app, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.
2.  Verify your mobile number. The app sends an SMS from your phone to confirm it’s your SIM.
3.  Choose your **bank**. The app finds accounts linked to your mobile number.
4.  Select the account. You’ll get a **UPI ID** (like name@bank).
5.  Set your **UPI PIN**:
    -   **With debit card:** enter the last 6 digits and expiry date, then the OTP.
    -   **With Aadhaar:** choose Aadhaar, enter the first 6 digits, and verify with the Aadhaar OTP and your bank OTP.
6.  Create a 4- or 6-digit UPI PIN and confirm it.

## UPI Lite: small payments without a PIN

**UPI Lite** is an on-device wallet for small payments. You load a small amount from your bank and pay low-value amounts **without entering your UPI PIN**. Enable it in apps that support it, such as BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm. Per-payment and wallet limits are set by NPCI and shown in the app.

## Safety rules

-   You **never need a PIN to receive money**. If someone asks you to enter your PIN “to receive”, it’s a scam.
-   Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.
-   Report fraud on **1930** or [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).
