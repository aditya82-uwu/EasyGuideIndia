**Aadhaar seeding** (or Aadhaar–bank linking / NPCI mapping) is what allows government benefits (DBT) such as PM-Kisan, scholarships, LPG subsidy and pensions to be paid directly into your bank account using your Aadhaar number. If a payment has “failed” or “Aadhaar not seeded”, this is the first thing to check.

## How to check your Aadhaar bank seeding status

1.  Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP.
2.  Select **Bank Seeding Status**.
3.  You’ll see the **bank name** your Aadhaar is currently mapped to, the status (active/inactive) and the date it was seeded.

This shows the **one bank account** that receives Aadhaar-based DBT payments, which may not be every account you’ve given your Aadhaar to.

## Aadhaar not seeded, or the wrong bank?

1.  Visit your bank branch (or use net banking, if your bank offers it) and ask for **Aadhaar seeding / NPCI mapping** for DBT.
2.  Fill in the Aadhaar seeding consent form and submit a copy of your Aadhaar.
3.  The bank sends the request to NPCI. It can take a few working days to reflect.
4.  Check the status again on myAadhaar.

Many banks and India Post Payments Bank also let you seed Aadhaar at a branch or with a postman.

## Aadhaar seeding vs Aadhaar linking

**Linking** Aadhaar to an account (for KYC) isn’t the same as **seeding** it in the NPCI mapper for DBT. Your Aadhaar can be linked to several accounts, but DBT goes only to the account shown in the seeding status. For PM-Kisan, see our [PM Kisan status guide](/documents/pm-kisan-status-check/).
