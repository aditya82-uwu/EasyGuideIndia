You can change the address on your Aadhaar card online through UIDAI’s **myAadhaar** portal, without visiting an Aadhaar centre, as long as your mobile number is linked to your Aadhaar. You just log in with an OTP, upload a proof of address, and track the request with the number you get. Below are the exact steps, accepted documents, fees and what to do if your request is rejected.

## What you need

-   Your **Aadhaar number** and access to the **mobile number registered with Aadhaar** (for the OTP)
-   A clear scan or photo of a valid **Proof of Address (PoA)** in your name, in JPEG, PNG or PDF format (under 2 MB)
-   The address exactly as it appears on the document

## How to update your Aadhaar address online

1.  Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and click **Login**.
2.  Enter your Aadhaar number and the captcha, then the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
3.  Choose **Address Update** (or Document Update if you’re refreshing your proofs).
4.  Select **Update Aadhaar Online**, then choose **Address** as the field to update.
5.  Enter your new address carefully. It must match the document you’re uploading.
6.  Select your document type from the list and upload a clear copy.
7.  Pay the fee if one applies (see below), then submit.
8.  Note the **URN / SRN (Update/Service Request Number)** shown on screen and in the SMS. You’ll need it to track the update.

## Accepted proof of address documents

UIDAI accepts a long list of documents. The most commonly used are:

-   Passport
-   Bank passbook or bank statement (with bank stamp)
-   Voter ID card
-   Driving licence
-   Ration card
-   Electricity, water, gas or landline bill (usually not older than 3 months)
-   Registered rent or lease agreement

The document must be in **your name**. If you don’t have one, you can use the Head of Family (HoF) option on myAadhaar, where a family member whose Aadhaar already has the address approves your update, or visit an Aadhaar centre.

## How long does it take?

Most online address updates are processed within a few days to a few weeks. UIDAI can take up to 90 days in some cases. You can [check your Aadhaar update status](/documents/check-aadhaar-update-status/) with your URN at any time. Once it’s approved, [download your updated e-Aadhaar](/documents/download-aadhaar-card-online/) or [order a PVC card](/documents/order-aadhaar-pvc-card-online/).

## Common reasons updates get rejected

-   The address typed doesn’t match the uploaded document.
-   The document is blurred, cropped or not in your name.
-   The bill or statement is too old.
-   You uploaded a document type that isn’t valid as proof of address.

If your request is rejected, the SMS or status page usually shows the reason. Fix it and submit a fresh request.
