Money debited but the UPI payment, card payment or IMPS transfer failed? Under RBI’s rules on failed transactions, your bank or payment provider must **reverse the money within a fixed time**, and pay **₹100 per day** of delay after that. Here are the deadlines and how to complain.

## RBI refund deadlines for failed transactions

| Type of transaction | Must be reversed within | Compensation if late |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPI / IMPS: debited but not credited to the receiver | T+1 day | ₹100 per day |
| Card at shop (POS) or online: debited but merchant not paid | T+5 days | ₹100 per day |
| Card-to-card transfer failed | T+1 day | ₹100 per day |
| ATM cash not dispensed | T+5 days | ₹100 per day |

T = the day of the transaction. Compensation is meant to be paid automatically.

## What to do

1.  **Don’t pay again straight away.** Check whether the first payment went through.
2.  **Raise a dispute in the app.** Every UPI app has a Help / Raise Complaint option on the transaction. For card payments, contact your card issuer.
3.  **Complain to your bank** if it isn’t resolved in the time above. Keep the transaction ID / UTR number.
4.  **Escalate to the RBI Ombudsman** at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in/) if there’s no resolution within 30 days. For UPI, you can also use NPCI’s dispute redressal on its website.

## If it’s fraud, not a failure

If the payment was unauthorised, report it immediately on **1930** or [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) and inform your bank in writing. Quick reporting limits your liability under RBI rules.
