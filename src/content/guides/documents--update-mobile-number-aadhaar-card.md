Your mobile number is the key to almost every Aadhaar service online: OTPs for downloads, updates, [DigiLocker](/documents/download-documents-from-digilocker/), bank KYC and more. If you’ve changed your number, or never linked one, here are the two ways to update it: at an Aadhaar centre, or through UIDAI’s official Aadhaar app using face authentication.

## Fee

UIDAI’s rate card charges **₹75** for a demographic update, which includes a mobile number change.

## Option 1: Update at an Aadhaar centre

1.  Find your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or enrolment centre on the UIDAI website, or book an appointment on myAadhaar.
2.  Fill in the Aadhaar update/correction form and write the new mobile number.
3.  Verify with your fingerprint or iris. **No documents are needed** for a mobile number update.
4.  Pay ₹75 and keep the acknowledgement slip with the URN.
5.  The new number is usually active within a few days.

## Option 2: Update in the Aadhaar app (face authentication)

UIDAI’s official **Aadhaar app** now allows mobile number updates from home using OTP plus **face authentication**, where a live selfie is matched against your Aadhaar photo. Install the app only from the official Play Store or App Store listing by UIDAI, open the update option, and follow the prompts.

If your old registered number isn’t accessible, the app may direct you to a centre or other assisted service instead.

## Check that it’s updated

Use **Verify Email/Mobile Number** on [myAadhaar](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/), or [check your update status](/documents/check-aadhaar-update-status/) with the URN.
