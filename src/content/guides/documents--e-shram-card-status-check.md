The **e-Shram card** is the Ministry of Labour’s registration for unorganised workers: daily-wage labourers, domestic workers, street vendors, gig and platform workers, farm workers and more. Once registered, you get a **12-digit UAN (Universal Account Number)**. Here’s how to check your registration, download your card, and what the card actually gives you.

## Important: e-Shram does not pay a monthly amount

Many websites and videos talk about an “e-Shram payment status” or “₹1,000 monthly”. The e-Shram card itself **does not provide a regular payment**. It’s a national database that helps you access social security schemes. Some state governments have made one-time payments to registered workers in the past, but these were state schemes announced separately. Be careful of anyone asking for money or OTPs to “release” an e-Shram payment.

## How to check your e-Shram registration and download your card

1.  Go to [eshram.gov.in](https://eshram.gov.in/).
2.  Click **Already Registered? Update / Login**.
3.  Enter your Aadhaar-linked mobile number or UAN and verify with the OTP.
4.  Your profile opens. You can view details, update information and **download the UAN card**.

## What benefits are linked to e-Shram?

-   **Accident insurance:** through PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY), subject to scheme conditions.
-   **Access to schemes:** e-Shram data is used to connect workers with central and state welfare schemes. The portal lists schemes you may qualify for.
-   A **UAN** recognised across states, useful if you migrate for work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I check if my e-Shram card is active?

Log in on eshram.gov.in with your mobile or UAN. If your profile and UAN card open, your registration is active.

### I forgot my UAN. What do I do?

Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number and OTP on eshram.gov.in, where your UAN is shown on your profile and card.

### Is there an e-Shram helpline?

Yes. The national helpline number **14434** is listed on the e-Shram portal.
