The **Aadhaar PVC card** is a durable, wallet-size plastic card with a QR code, hologram and your photo, printed by UIDAI and sent to your address by Speed Post. You can order it online for **₹75** (revised from ₹50 in January 2026), even if your mobile number isn’t registered with Aadhaar.

## Aadhaar PVC card fee

<table><tbody><tr><td><strong>Fee</strong></td><td>₹75 (including GST and Speed Post), revised from ₹50 effective January 2026</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Payment</strong></td><td>Online: UPI, debit/credit card or net banking</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Delivery</strong></td><td>By Speed Post to the address on your Aadhaar</td></tr></tbody></table>

## How to order the Aadhaar PVC card

1.  Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and choose **Order Aadhaar PVC Card**.
2.  Enter your **Aadhaar number, VID or EID**, and the captcha.
3.  Request the OTP. If your mobile isn’t registered with Aadhaar, tick the option for a **non-registered / alternate mobile number** and enter that number.
4.  Enter the OTP. With a registered mobile, you’ll see a preview of the details that will be printed.
5.  Pay ₹75 online.
6.  Save the **SRN (Service Request Number)** and payment receipt. You’ll get an SMS once it’s dispatched, with a Speed Post tracking number.

## Important things to know

-   The card is printed with the details **currently in your Aadhaar**. If your address or photo is wrong, [update it first](/documents/update-aadhaar-address-online/), wait for approval, then order.
-   It’s delivered to the address in your Aadhaar record, not to a different address.
-   Only order from the official myAadhaar portal or Aadhaar app. Plastic “Aadhaar cards” printed by private shops aren’t official.

## Track your PVC card order

On myAadhaar, use **Check Aadhaar PVC Card Status** with your SRN. Once it’s dispatched, track it on India Post’s website using the Speed Post number in your SMS.
