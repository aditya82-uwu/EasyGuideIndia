**Tatkal** is Indian Railways’ quota for last-minute travel. Bookings open **one day before the journey**, and seats on popular trains sell out in minutes. Since July 2025, **Aadhaar OTP authentication is mandatory** for online Tatkal bookings. Here are the timings, rules and a routine that gives you the best chance of a confirmed ticket.

## Tatkal booking timings

| Class | Booking opens |
| --- | --- |
| AC classes (1A, 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC) | 10:00 AM, one day before the journey |
| Non-AC classes (SL, 2S, FC) | 11:00 AM, one day before the journey |

“One day before” is counted from the date the train leaves its origin station, excluding the journey date.

## Key Tatkal rules

-   **Aadhaar OTP:** your IRCTC account must be **Aadhaar-authenticated**, and an OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile during online Tatkal booking.
-   **Up to 4 passengers** per PNR.
-   **Agents** can’t book Tatkal in the first 30 minutes of the opening window.
-   **Confirmed Tatkal tickets are not refundable** on cancellation (except in cases like train cancellation).
-   **Tatkal charges** are added to the base fare and depend on the class, with a minimum and maximum. IRCTC shows the exact amount.

## How to prepare the day before

1.  **Aadhaar-verify your IRCTC account** (My Account → Authenticate User) well in advance.
2.  **Create a Master List** of passengers (My Profile → Master List) so you don’t type names during the rush.
3.  Keep your **UPI app or payment method ready**. UPI and IRCTC eWallet are usually fastest.
4.  Note the **train number, class and boarding station**.

## Booking steps on the day

1.  Log in a few minutes before 10:00 / 11:00 AM on the IRCTC website or the IRCTC Rail Connect app.
2.  Search your route and date, and select **TATKAL** quota.
3.  At opening time, choose the train and class and click **Book Now**.
4.  Add passengers from your Master List and complete the **Aadhaar OTP**.
5.  Pay quickly. Seats are held only for a short time.
