You can cancel an IRCTC e-ticket online in under a minute, on the IRCTC website or the **IRCTC Rail Connect** app. The refund goes back to your payment method. Counter tickets can also be cancelled online if you have the PNR and the mobile number used during booking.

## Cancel an e-ticket (booked online)

1.  Log in at [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) or in the IRCTC Rail Connect app.
2.  Go to **My Account → My Transactions → Booked Ticket History** (in the app: My Bookings).
3.  Select the ticket and click **Cancel Ticket**.
4.  Tick the passengers you want to cancel. You can cancel some and keep others.
5.  Check the **refund amount** shown and confirm.
6.  You’ll get an SMS and email with the refund details.

## Cancel a counter (PRS) ticket online

1.  On IRCTC, use **Cancel Ticket → Counter Ticket**.
2.  Enter the PNR, train number and captcha, then the OTP sent to the mobile given when booking.
3.  Confirm the cancellation, then collect the refund at a reservation counter within the allowed time, taking the original ticket.

## How much will I get back?

It depends on how early you cancel and the ticket status. See the full [IRCTC refund rules](/railway/irctc-refund-rules/). In short: cancelling more than 72 hours before departure costs only a flat charge, while cancelling less than 8 hours before gives no refund on a confirmed ticket.
