Want to know exactly where your train is and how late it’s running? Indian Railways’ **NTES (National Train Enquiry System)** shows live running status, expected arrival and delays for every train. Here’s how to use it, plus offline options.

## How to check live train running status online

1.  Open the official [NTES / Indian Railways enquiry](https://enquiry.indianrail.gov.in/) website, or the **NTES app**.
2.  Choose **Spot Your Train** / Live Train Running Status.
3.  Enter the **train number or name** and select the journey date.
4.  You’ll see the last station passed, the delay, and expected arrival times at upcoming stations.

## Without internet

SMS **SPOT <train number>** to **139**, or call 139. See [train running status by SMS](/railway/train-running-status-sms/).

## Tips

-   Choose the **correct start date**. For long-distance trains it’s the date the train left its origin station, which may be a day before you board.
-   Check the platform number on NTES or station displays close to arrival, as platforms can change.

## If the status looks wrong

-   **"Train not found" or no data.** Check the train number and start date again. A train that left its origin yesterday needs yesterday's date.
-   **Delay does not change.** Status updates as the train passes stations that report to the system, so it can stay the same between stations. Check again after the next stop.
-   **Cancelled or rescheduled trains.** NTES shows cancellations, diversions and rescheduled departures. Check it again before you leave home for the station.
-   **Different apps, different numbers.** Third-party apps estimate arrival times. For a decision about leaving home, trust the NTES figure and the station display.
