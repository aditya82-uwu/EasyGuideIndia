The **IPL playoffs** decide the champion from the top four teams of the league stage. The top two get two chances to reach the final, while the third- and fourth-placed teams must win twice in a row. This guide explains the format, how the points table and net run rate work, and what happens if rain hits a playoff or the final.

## League stage and points table

-   **10 teams** each play **14 league matches**.
-   A win is worth **2 points**. A no-result or abandoned match gives **1 point** each. A loss gives 0. A tie is settled by a Super Over, so there’s a winner.
-   Teams level on points are separated by **net run rate (NRR)**.
-   The **top four** teams qualify for the playoffs.

## How net run rate (NRR) works

**NRR = (runs scored ÷ overs faced) − (runs conceded ÷ overs bowled)**, across the whole season.

-   If a team is **bowled out**, it’s counted as having faced its full quota of overs (usually 20), not the overs it actually batted.
-   Winning big (a large run margin, or chasing quickly) raises NRR. Heavy defeats lower it.
-   In the final league games, teams chasing a playoff spot often calculate exactly how fast they must chase or how much they must win by to overtake a rival’s NRR.

## Playoff format

| Match | Teams | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualifier 1 | 1st vs 2nd | Winner goes to the final. Loser plays Qualifier 2 |
| Eliminator | 3rd vs 4th | Winner plays Qualifier 2. Loser is out |
| Qualifier 2 | Loser of Q1 vs winner of Eliminator | Winner goes to the final |
| Final | Winner of Q1 vs winner of Q2 | Champion |

That’s why finishing in the **top two** matters so much: a team that loses Qualifier 1 still gets another chance in Qualifier 2.

## What happens if it rains in the playoffs?

-   **Extra time:** Qualifier 1, the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 have extra time (about 120 minutes) built in, so overs are reduced only after that is used up.
-   **Minimum overs:** a result needs at least **5 overs per side**.
-   **Super Over:** if a playoff match is tied, or can’t be completed but a Super Over is possible, the Super Over decides it.
-   **No play possible:** if not even a Super Over can be played, the team that finished **higher in the league table** goes through.
-   **Final:** the final has a **reserve day**. If no result is possible on the reserve day either, the team placed higher in the league stage is declared champion.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many teams qualify for the IPL playoffs?

Four: the top four teams in the league table.

### Why do the top two teams have an advantage?

They play Qualifier 1, and the loser still gets a second chance in Qualifier 2. Third and fourth must win both the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 to reach the final.

### Is there a reserve day for Qualifier 1 or the Eliminator?

No. Only the final has a reserve day. The other playoff matches use extra time, then a Super Over, then league position.
