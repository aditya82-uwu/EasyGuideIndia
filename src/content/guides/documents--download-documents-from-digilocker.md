**DigiLocker** is the Government of India’s free digital document wallet. Documents in its **Issued Documents** section come directly from the issuing department: Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, vehicle RC, CBSE and many state board marksheets, and more. Under the IT Act rules, they’re treated as **equivalent to the originals**. Here’s how to set up DigiLocker and download your documents.

## How to create a DigiLocker account

1.  Open [digilocker.gov.in](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/) or install the official **DigiLocker** app.
2.  Click **Sign Up** and enter your name, date of birth, gender, mobile number and a 6-digit security PIN.
3.  Verify your mobile with the OTP.
4.  Link your **Aadhaar** (OTP verification). This lets DigiLocker fetch documents issued in your name.

## How to get and download documents

1.  Log in and go to **Search Documents** (or Pull Partner Documents).
2.  Search for the document, for example “PAN”, “Driving Licence” or “Class X Marksheet”.
3.  Choose the issuer, enter the details asked (such as DL number or roll number), and click **Get Document**.
4.  The document appears under **Issued Documents**. Open it to view, download the PDF or share it.

## Popular documents you can get

| Document | Issuer |
| --- | --- |
| Aadhaar | UIDAI |
| PAN verification record | Income Tax Department |
| Driving licence and vehicle RC | Ministry of Road Transport & Highways |
| Class 10/12 marksheets and certificates | CBSE and many state boards |
| Ration card, caste/income certificates | Participating state departments |

## Issued vs uploaded documents

**Issued documents** come straight from the department and are valid as originals. **Uploaded documents** are your own scans. They’re convenient to store, but they don’t have the same legal status.
