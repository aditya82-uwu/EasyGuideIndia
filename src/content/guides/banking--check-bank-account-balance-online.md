With internet access, the quickest ways to check your bank balance are your **bank’s mobile app**, **net banking** or any **UPI app**. Here’s how each works. For options without internet, see [checking your balance without an app](/banking/check-bank-balance-without-app/).

## Method 1: UPI apps (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)

1.  Open the UPI app linked to your bank account.
2.  Tap **Check Balance** (usually under your profile or bank account).
3.  Select the account and enter your **UPI PIN**.
4.  Your available balance appears on screen.

## Method 2: Your bank’s mobile app

Log in with your MPIN, fingerprint or face ID. The balance is usually shown on the home screen, along with your mini-statement.

## Method 3: Net banking

1.  Go to your bank’s official net banking website. Type the address yourself; don’t use links from messages.
2.  Log in with your user ID and password (plus OTP if asked).
3.  Open **Accounts → Account Summary** to see your balance and download statements.

## Stay safe

-   Checking your balance never requires sharing your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
-   Use only official apps from the Play Store or App Store.
-   If you see a debit you don’t recognise, block the account in your app and report cyber fraud on **1930**.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does checking balance on UPI cost anything?

No. Balance enquiry on UPI is free.
