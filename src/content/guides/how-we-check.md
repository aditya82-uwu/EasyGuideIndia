Easy Guide India publishes match-day guides for Indian cricket grounds and plain-language explainers on IPL rules, railways, banking and documents. This page explains how a guide gets from draft to published, and where its limits are.

## How guides are written

Drafts are prepared with AI tools. Before a page is published, and again at each update, its facts are checked against the sources listed below. A figure we cannot confirm is either removed or worded as "commonly", "reported" or "in earlier seasons".

## What we check against

- Official pages from teams, boards, grounds and government bodies, where they are reachable.
- Police and traffic advisories, as reported by established news outlets.
- Recent reporting from established news outlets for fees, deadlines and rule changes.
- For fares, fees and tax rules, the notice of the body that issues them, where we can find it.

## What we do not claim

- There is no single official list of banned items for every IPL ground, so our lists show what has appeared in police and club notices in recent seasons.
- We do not say a rule applies to IPL 2027 until a notice for that season is published. Pages built from IPL 2026 notices say so.
- Parking, road closures and gate times change from match to match. Treat our guides as a starting point and confirm on your ticket and the notice for your match.

## When pages change

The quick-answers panel on a ground guide shows the month it was last checked. Before IPL 2027 we re-check each ground guide against the team's match-day notices, and we update the [IPL 2027 stadium guide](/ipl-2027/) as the schedule and venues are confirmed.

## Report a mistake

If you find a wrong fact, a broken link or an outdated rule, tell us through the [contact page](/contact-us/). Include the page address and, if you can, a link to the notice that shows the correct detail. We correct confirmed errors and update the "last checked" date.

Easy Guide India is an independent site. It is not affiliated with the BCCI, the IPL, any franchise, any stadium or any government body.
