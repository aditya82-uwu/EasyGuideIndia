To book train tickets online, you need an **IRCTC account**. Registration is free. Once you **Aadhaar-authenticate** your account, you can book Tatkal tickets and get priority in the first window when general bookings open. Here’s how to register, log in and book.

## Create an IRCTC account

1.  Go to [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) or the **IRCTC Rail Connect** app and click **Register**.
2.  Choose a username and password, and fill in your name, date of birth, email and mobile number.
3.  Verify your mobile and email with OTPs.

## Aadhaar-authenticate your account (recommended)

1.  After logging in, go to **My Account → Authenticate User**.
2.  Enter your Aadhaar number and verify with the Aadhaar OTP.

Aadhaar authentication is required for **online Tatkal bookings**. Indian Railways has also reserved the opening window of general reservations for Aadhaar-authenticated users.

## How to book a ticket

1.  Log in and enter From, To, date and quota, then search.
2.  Choose the train and class and check [seat availability](/railway/check-train-seat-availability-online/).
3.  Click **Book Now** and add passenger details (or pick from your Master List).
4.  Choose your boarding station and preferences, then pay.
5.  Your e-ticket and PNR are sent by SMS and email.

## Login problems

-   **Forgot password:** use Forgot Password on the login page (OTP to your registered mobile or email).
-   **Account locked:** contact IRCTC customer care through the website.
