Driving from Bandra to Wankhede on a match day is the slowest option. The ground has no public parking, and police divert traffic around Marine Drive and Churchgate before and after IPL matches. The easier plan is to leave the car at home and use the Western line or the Aqua Line metro.

## The short answer

From Bandra, Andheri and anywhere else on the Western line, take a local train to **Churchgate**, the terminus. The stadium is a few minutes walk from the station. **Marine Lines**, one stop before Churchgate, is also within walking distance and can be less crowded.

## From Bandra

-   **Local train.** Board any Churchgate-bound train at Bandra. Fast trains skip stations and are quicker, but they fill up first on match evenings. Third-party journey planners put the trip at roughly half an hour to 40 minutes, so treat that as an estimate and check the timetable on the day.
-   **Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line).** Bandra Colony and Bandra Kurla Complex are stations on this underground line, which also has a Churchgate station. The full line to Cuffe Parade opened on 8 October 2025. If you live or work near BKC, it can save you the walk or auto ride to Bandra station. Check the first and last train times with the operator, Mumbai Metro Rail Corporation, before you rely on a late return.
-   **Cab.** Possible, but expect diversions near Marine Drive and a long wait to leave after the match. If you take one, get dropped a little away from the ground and walk the last stretch.

## From Andheri, Borivali and the rest of the Western line

Take any train towards Churchgate. Fast and slow trains both end there. Avoid the last train you can catch before gates open, because station exits and the walk to the ground are slow when crowds arrive together.

## From Thane, Navi Mumbai and the Central line

Central Railway and Harbour line trains end at **CSMT**. From there it is about a 20 to 25 minute walk, or a short cab ride, to Wankhede. Another route is to change to the Western line at a suitable station and ride to Churchgate, but check which interchange suits you on the day.

## Getting home after the match

-   Expect a very full Churchgate station for the first 30 to 45 minutes after the final ball. Waiting a little, or walking to Marine Lines, can be faster than queuing at Churchgate.
-   Check the last local train and the last metro train before you go in. Match nights can run late.
-   If you plan a cab, book from a spot away from the stadium. Pickup near the main gates is slow and prices are often higher.
