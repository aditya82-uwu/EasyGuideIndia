Your Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) balance is yours to check at any time. You do not need to ask your employer. What you do need is an activated Universal Account Number (UAN) and a mobile number registered with it.

## What you need first

-   **UAN.** A 12-digit number that stays with you across jobs. It is on your payslip or with your HR team.
-   **UAN activated.** Activate it on the EPFO member portal with your UAN, Aadhaar-linked details and an OTP.
-   **Registered mobile number.** Missed call and SMS work only from the number registered with your UAN. If it has changed, update it on the portal.
-   **KYC seeded.** Aadhaar, bank account and PAN should be linked to your UAN. Without KYC, some services fail.

## Check on the EPFO member passbook

1.  Open the [EPFO member passbook](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/MemberPassBook/login) site.
2.  Log in with your UAN, password and captcha. If you have not set a password, activate your UAN on the [member portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/) first.
3.  Pick the member ID for the employer you want. The passbook shows each contribution and the closing balance.

## Check by missed call

Give a missed call from your registered mobile number to **9966044425**. The call disconnects on its own, and you get an SMS with your balance and last contribution. Secondary sources agree on this number but we could not confirm it on the EPFO site, so check there if it fails.

## Check by SMS

Several sources give an SMS format of **EPFOHO UAN** sent to **7738299899**, with a language code at the end if you want a reply in a language other than English. Confirm the number and format on the EPFO site before relying on it.

## Check on the UMANG app

Install the UMANG app, search for EPFO, choose the passbook or balance option and log in with your UAN and OTP.

## If the balance looks wrong

-   **Employer contribution missing.** Contributions usually appear a few weeks after the month ends. If a month is missing for longer, ask your employer first, then use the grievance option on the EPFO portal.
-   **Old PF accounts.** If you changed jobs, you may have more than one member ID. Check each one, and merge them with a transfer request when the details match.
-   **Name or date of birth mismatch.** A mismatch with Aadhaar blocks KYC and claims. Ask your employer to correct it through the portal.

## Keep your account safe

EPFO does not ask for your OTP or password by phone or WhatsApp. Do not share them with anyone who offers to release or unlock your PF.
