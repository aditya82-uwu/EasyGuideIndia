Applied for a new PAN or a correction and want to know where it is? You can track it online with your **acknowledgement number**. Where you check depends on where you applied: the Income Tax portal (instant e-PAN), Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL.

## Instant e-PAN (Income Tax portal)

1.  Go to the [Income Tax e-filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/) and click **Instant e-PAN**.
2.  Choose **Check Status / Download PAN**.
3.  Enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP.
4.  You’ll see whether the PAN is allotted, and can download the e-PAN.

## Protean (NSDL) application

1.  Open the Protean PAN status page (linked from the [Income Tax Department’s status page](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/tax-services/status-of-pan-protean)).
2.  Select **PAN – New / Change Request**.
3.  Enter your 15-digit **acknowledgement number** and the captcha.
4.  Submit to see the current status.

## UTIITSL application

1.  Go to the UTIITSL PAN portal and choose **Track PAN Card**.
2.  Enter your **application coupon number** or PAN, and your date of birth.
3.  Submit to see the status.

## Common statuses

| Status | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Under process / application received | Wait; this is normal for the first few days |
| PAN allotted | Your PAN number is ready. Download the e-PAN; the physical card follows by post. |
| Dispatched | Track the card with the India Post consignment number |
| Discrepancy / objection | Respond with the requested document or correction |
