Your **Aadhaar authentication history** shows every time your Aadhaar was used for verification: by a bank, SIM provider, ration shop, AePS agent or government service. It shows the date, time, type (OTP, fingerprint, iris, face or demographic) and the agency. Checking it regularly is the fastest way to spot misuse.

## How to check your Aadhaar authentication history

1.  Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP.
2.  Select **Authentication History**.
3.  Choose the **authentication type** (all, biometric, OTP, demographic, etc.) and the **date range**.
4.  Click **Fetch Authentication History** to see the records.

UIDAI shows up to the last six months of records, a limited number at a time.

## What each record shows

-   Date and time of authentication
-   Modality: OTP, fingerprint, iris, face or demographic
-   Name of the Authentication User Agency (AUA), for example a bank or telecom company
-   Whether it succeeded or failed, and a reference code

## Found an entry you don’t recognise?

1.  [Lock your Aadhaar biometrics](/documents/check-aadhaar-update-status/) immediately.
2.  Report it to UIDAI by calling **1947** or emailing **help@uidai.gov.in**.
3.  If money was withdrawn through AePS, inform your bank at once and report cyber fraud on **1930** or [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/).
