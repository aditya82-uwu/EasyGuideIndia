Need to board your train at a different station than the one on your ticket? You can change your **boarding point** online on IRCTC. News reports say rules revised in April 2026 let you do it **up to 30 minutes before the train leaves its originating station** (earlier, only until chart preparation). IRCTC’s own help page had not caught up when those reports appeared, so confirm the deadline for your PNR in your booking history.

## Rules for changing the boarding station

-   Allowed for **confirmed and RAC** tickets; **not for waitlisted** tickets.
-   You can change the boarding point **only once** per ticket.
-   The new boarding station must be **on the route** between the original boarding and destination stations.
-   Once changed, you **can’t board at the original station**, and the seat can be given to others if you don’t board at the new one.
-   No refund of the fare for the section you don’t travel.

## How to change your boarding station on IRCTC

1.  Log in at [irctc.co.in](https://www.irctc.co.in/) or the IRCTC Rail Connect app.
2.  Go to **My Transactions → Booked Ticket History** and select the ticket.
3.  Click **Change Boarding Point**.
4.  Pick the new station from the list and confirm.
5.  You’ll get an SMS confirming the change.
