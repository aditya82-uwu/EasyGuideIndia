The **Digital Rupee (e₹)** is India’s central bank digital currency (CBDC), digital cash issued by the Reserve Bank of India. Unlike UPI, which moves money between bank accounts, e₹ is held as **digital tokens in a wallet** on your phone, like cash in a digital purse. The retail pilot runs through participating banks’ e₹ wallet apps. Here’s how it works.

## e₹ vs UPI

|  | Digital Rupee (e₹) | UPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Digital currency issued by RBI | A payment system that moves money between bank accounts |
| Where money sits | In your e₹ wallet (like cash) | In your bank account |
| Interest | None (like cash) | Your savings account earns interest |
| Paying at shops | Scan UPI QR codes (interoperable) or e₹ QR | Scan UPI QR codes |

## How to start using the Digital Rupee

1.  Check whether your bank offers an **e₹ wallet** in the retail CBDC pilot (for example SBI, ICICI, HDFC, Union Bank, Bank of Baroda and others have launched apps).
2.  Download your bank’s official **Digital Rupee app** from the Play Store or App Store.
3.  Register with your mobile number linked to your bank account and set a wallet PIN.
4.  **Load** e₹ into the wallet from your bank account.
5.  **Pay** by scanning a merchant QR code, or send e₹ to another person’s wallet.
6.  **Redeem** unused e₹ back to your bank account at any time.

## Is it safe?

-   e₹ is issued by RBI and is legal tender.
-   Your wallet is protected by a PIN. Keep your phone secure, as losing the wallet is like losing cash unless your bank app lets you recover it.
-   Use only your bank’s official app.
