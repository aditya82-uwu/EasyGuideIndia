Want a clearer photo or an updated signature on your PAN card? Changes like this are made through a **“Changes or Correction in existing PAN data / Reprint of PAN Card”** request with Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL. You keep the same PAN number and get a new card with the updated details.

## Two ways to apply

| Method | How the photo and signature are updated |
| --- | --- |
| Aadhaar e-KYC and e-Sign (paperless) | The photo on your PAN is taken from your **Aadhaar** records. There’s no separate photo upload, so update your Aadhaar photo first if you want a new one. |
| Scanned upload / physical documents | Upload a recent photo and signature as specified on the form, or send the signed form with a photograph to the agency. |

## Steps (Protean/NSDL example)

1.  Open Protean’s PAN application page and choose **Changes or Correction in existing PAN data / Reprint of PAN card**.
2.  Enter your PAN and details, and choose how to submit (Aadhaar e-KYC or scanned documents).
3.  Tick the **photo mismatch** and/or **signature mismatch** options in the form.
4.  Upload the required proofs, a photo and signature (for the scanned method).
5.  Pay the fee (similar to a new PAN application, about ₹107 for an Indian address).
6.  Save the acknowledgement number and [track the status](/documents/check-pan-card-status-online/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Will my PAN number change?

No. Only the card details are updated; the PAN number stays the same.

### Why is my Aadhaar photo showing on the new PAN?

If you apply via Aadhaar e-KYC, the photo is taken from your Aadhaar. To change it, update your Aadhaar photo at an Aadhaar centre first.
