No internet, no smartphone, or just don’t want to open an app? You can still check your bank balance in seconds with a **missed call**, the **\*99# USSD service** or **WhatsApp banking**. All you need is the **mobile number registered with your bank account**.

## Method 1: Missed call (fastest)

Give a missed call from your registered mobile number. The call disconnects and you get an SMS with your balance. It’s free.

## Method 2: \*99# USSD (works on any phone, no internet)

1.  Dial **\*99#** from your registered mobile number (works on basic phones too).
2.  Choose your language.
3.  Select **Check Balance**.
4.  Enter your **UPI PIN** when prompted, and your balance appears on screen.

\*99# is run by NPCI. It needs a UPI PIN set for your account (see [how to set up UPI](/banking/upi-setup-guide-india-aadhaar-debit-card/)).

## Method 3: WhatsApp banking

Many banks offer balance enquiry on WhatsApp. Save your bank’s official WhatsApp number, send “Hi” from your registered mobile and choose Balance Enquiry. Examples: SBI **90226 90226**, HDFC Bank **70700 22222**, ICICI Bank **86400 86400**. Only use numbers listed on your bank’s official website.

## No SMS arriving? Troubleshooting

-   Call from the mobile number **registered with your account**. Update it at your branch if needed.
-   Register for missed-call banking if your bank requires it.
-   Check that SMS alerts are active and your inbox isn’t full.
-   For \*99#, make sure your UPI PIN is set.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is missed-call balance enquiry free?

Yes. The call disconnects automatically and banks don’t charge for it.

### Can I check my balance without a registered mobile number?

Not by phone. Use an ATM, your passbook at the branch, or net banking.

### Will the bank ask for my PIN or OTP on a missed call?

Never. Balance enquiry by missed call needs no PIN or OTP. Anyone calling you to ask for them is a fraudster.
