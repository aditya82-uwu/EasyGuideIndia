IPL 2027 is reported to start around 10 March 2027, and entry rules for the new season have not been published yet. The rules below come from IPL 2026 notices and are broadly the same across grounds: carry a valid ticket on your phone, use the gate printed on it, arrive early, and leave power banks, bags and outside food at home. We update this page when 2027 notices appear, and link to a guide for each ground.

_Rules are set by each franchise, state association and local police, and can change during the season. Always read the instructions on your ticket._

## Tickets and ID

-   **Buy only from the franchise’s official ticketing partner** (for IPL 2026, District for Rajasthan Royals and BookMyShow for Mumbai Indians; it differs by team and season). Tickets from resellers can be cancelled.
-   **Most IPL tickets are now digital**, with a QR code in the ticketing app that’s scanned at the gate. Mumbai Indians did not accept screenshots or screen recordings of its M-ticket in 2025, and Rajasthan Royals tickets sit in the District app profile and can’t be downloaded. Some guides say a few venues still ask fans to swap an e-ticket for a physical pass, so read your ticket instructions and keep your phone charged.
-   **Use the gate printed on your ticket.** Gates are spread around each stadium, sometimes on different roads.
-   **Carry a government photo ID** (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, or the [DigiLocker](/documents/download-documents-from-digilocker/) version) in case it’s asked for at security.
-   **Children:** Rajasthan Royals says a ticket is needed from age two, and anyone under 18 must come with an adult who also has a ticket. Other franchises may differ, so check the terms on the ticketing page before buying.

## Things not allowed in IPL stadiums

These items have appeared on prohibited lists at IPL venues in recent seasons, including Wankhede and Uppal. We found no official IPL 2026 list for most grounds, so use this as a guide and check your ticket:

-   **Power banks, chargers and cables**, and at several venues **earphones**, laptops, tablets and other gadgets
-   **Bags and backpacks** (some venues allow only a small purse or clutch)
-   **Outside food, water bottles**, cans and thermos flasks
-   **Helmets and umbrellas**
-   **Coins**, metal objects and sharp items
-   **Cigarettes, lighters, matchboxes**, gutka and pan masala
-   **Cameras**, binoculars, selfie sticks and laser pointers
-   Banners, flags on sticks, firecrackers and musical instruments

Cloakrooms are rare, so leave banned items at home. Items found at the gate may be confiscated.

## When to reach the stadium

Gates usually open about **3 hours before the first ball**. In IPL 2026, Jaipur, Mullanpur and Ahmedabad opened gates at 4:30 PM for a 7:30 PM match, and Chinnaswamy was reported to open about four hours before. Security has two layers at most grounds, so aim to arrive at least 90 minutes before the start.
