Not sure whether your PAN is linked with Aadhaar? It matters: **PANs issued with an Aadhaar enrolment ID (before 1 October 2024) had to be linked by 31 December 2025, or they became inoperative from 1 January 2026**. Other PANs had a 2023 deadline and a ₹1,000 late fee, per CBDT notifications. You can check your link status in under a minute online or by SMS, without logging in.

## Method 1: Income Tax e-filing portal

1.  Go to the [Income Tax e-filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/).
2.  Under Quick Links, click **Link Aadhaar Status**.
3.  Enter your **PAN** and **Aadhaar number**.
4.  Click **View Link Aadhaar Status**.

## Method 2: SMS

Send **UIDPAN <12-digit Aadhaar> <10-digit PAN>** to **567678** or **56161** from any mobile. You’ll get a reply saying whether they’re linked.

## What the result means

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your PAN is linked to Aadhaar | Nothing, you’re done |
| Link request sent to UIDAI for validation | Wait a few days and check again |
| PAN not linked with Aadhaar | Pay the ₹1,000 fee and [link it on the Income Tax portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/) |
