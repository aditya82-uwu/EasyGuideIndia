The **e-Aadhaar** is a digitally signed PDF copy of your Aadhaar that UIDAI says is **as valid as the physical card**. You can download it free, any number of times, from the myAadhaar portal, the official Aadhaar app or DigiLocker. Here’s how, plus how to open the password-protected PDF.

## How to download e-Aadhaar from myAadhaar

1.  Go to [myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in](https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/) and choose **Download Aadhaar**.
2.  Enter your **Aadhaar number**, **Enrolment ID (EID)** or **Virtual ID (VID)**, plus the captcha.
3.  Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
4.  Choose whether you want a **masked Aadhaar** (shows only the last 4 digits of your number, safer for sharing) and download the PDF.

## What is the e-Aadhaar PDF password?

The password is the **first four letters of your name in CAPITAL letters** followed by your **year of birth (YYYY)**.

| Name on Aadhaar | Year of birth | Password |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rahul Kumar | 1995 | RAHU1995 |
| Priya Sharma | 2001 | PRIY2001 |

If your first name has fewer than four letters, use all of it plus the year (for example, “Om” born 1990 = OM1990).

## Other ways to get your Aadhaar

-   **Aadhaar app / mAadhaar app:** log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP to view and share your Aadhaar.
-   **DigiLocker:** link your Aadhaar in [DigiLocker](/documents/download-documents-from-digilocker/) to get an e-Aadhaar in the Issued Documents section.
-   **PVC card:** for a wallet-size card by post, [order an Aadhaar PVC card](/documents/order-aadhaar-pvc-card-online/) (₹75).

## Can’t get the OTP?

If your mobile number isn’t registered with Aadhaar, you can’t download the e-Aadhaar online. Get your mobile number added first (see [how to update your mobile number in Aadhaar](/documents/update-mobile-number-aadhaar-card/)), or visit an Aadhaar centre.
